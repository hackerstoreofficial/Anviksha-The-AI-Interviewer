Our Story

Where Anviksha came from

The word Anviksha (अन्वीक्षा) comes from Sanskrit, meaning investigation or critical inquiry. It describes exactly what we set out to build — a tool that rigorously examines a candidate's knowledge, then reflects that analysis back as actionable growth.

"Most interview prep is either too generic, too expensive, or too stressful. We wanted to build the thing we wished we had when we were preparing."

Anviksha started as an experiment: could a locally-running, privacy-preserving AI genuinely simulate a technical interview well enough to be useful? After months of iteration — integrating speech recognition, real-time proctoring, multi-provider LLM routing, and resume OCR parsing — the answer turned out to be a resounding yes.

Today, Anviksha is a production-ready, fully open-source simulator that any developer, student, or hiring-prep platform can deploy and extend.
